# E-Commerce Pipeline Project

## Executive Summary
Currently being hampered by their high rate of incomplete orders, a fictional e-commerce company has approached me to perform a comprehensive analysis of their customer-base, orders
and product reviews in an attempt to become a major, global player in the highly competitive industry of online retail.

The goal of my analysis was to transform raw transactional and customer data into actionable insights that support data-driven decision making across customer engagement, sales
performance and product strategy. Simulating the SQL -> Excel -> Power BI workflow, this project presents a complete, end-to-end analytics pipeline that demostrates proficiency in
the various tools for everyday business insights.
